Tooltipに説明を入れ、
FunctionTextのKSに刀式、KAに秘剣を記載する。

	Tooltip: ""
	FunctionText:
		'''
		{$Tooltips.KS} <>
		- Fires a total of {0} feathers during a double jump.
		{$Tooltips.KA} <>
		- 
		'''

例：錫の刀の場合
TinKatana: {
	DisplayName: Tin Katana
	Tooltip: ""
	FunctionText:
		'''
		{$Tooltips.KS} [c/ADAC58:<Tin Protection>]
		- +{0} defense. If the ore type matches the armor, additional +{1} defense.
		{$Tooltips.KA} [c/FFFFFF:<Flash Attack>]
		- Dash and attack the enemy.
		'''
}
